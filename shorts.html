About the kids preset. Photoreal AI children draw heavy platform scrutiny, and anything YouTube reads as children's content gets flagged Made for Kids — which switches off comments, notifications and most monetisation. This preset is locked to a clearly cartoon style for that reason. Animal casts avoid the problem entirely and tend to travel further.

Why consistency is hard

Image models redraw from scratch every time. A fixed cast sheet plus a fixed seed gets you close — same breed, colours, proportions, style — but not identical. Treat it like a comic strip drawn by hand, not a rendered 3D character.

How a day runs

  1. Lock the cast once. Reuse it for weeks.
  2. Write clips — Hindi dialogue and panel prompts.
  3. Take: generate three panels, then film the ten seconds. Talk over it live if you want your own voice.
  4. Publish to Shorts. Instagram is a download-and-post step — see Setup for why.

Two or three clips a day is the sweet spot. Beyond that Shorts starts reading the channel as a content farm.

Instagram

This one can't be one click from a static page. Instagram's publishing API doesn't accept a file — it wants a public URL to fetch, in MP4 with H.264 and AAC. This app renders WebM in your browser with nowhere to host it. It also needs a Business account, a linked Facebook Page, a Meta app, and approved instagram_content_publish permission, which is a two-to-four week review.

The honest workflow: download the clip, then post it from your phone. Ten seconds of tapping, and the caption is already written for you.

Authorized JavaScript origin for the OAuth client:

Google Flow can't drive this. Flow's free daily credits only work inside Flow's own web interface — there's no public API, and Google bills Flow credits and API usage as separate systems, so the credits can't be spent programmatically. The Gemini and Vertex routes to Veo do have an API, but video generation there is paid per second with no free tier. Unofficial bridges that log into Flow for you exist, cost money, and break whenever Google changes the page.

So where do the pictures come from

Pollinations — free, no key, no account. It makes still panels, not motion, so this app animates them: slow push-ins, cuts on the dialogue beats, captions timed to the lines. It's a comic strip that moves rather than generated footage, and for ten-second comedy that difference matters less than you'd think.

If you ever want true Veo motion, generate the clips by hand in Flow with your free credits, download them, and post from your phone. This app won't get in the way.
